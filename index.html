Get an email when it matters

We'll email you only when a federal bill misaligned with the values you kept is new or moves forward (at most once every 3 days), with who represents ZIP and how to reach them. You'll confirm by email first, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Your email is used for nothing else.

How this works. Bills come from Congress.gov. Plain-English summaries and value scores are written by an AI model (Claude) from each bill's text and the Congressional Research Service summary, and can contain mistakes — always check the official bill page before acting. A bill's flag reflects only the values you keep checked: URGENT means it works against them overall, SAFE means it advances them, NEUTRAL means it doesn't move them either way. Bills that are already law or dead are left out.

Data: Congress.gov · House Clerk & Senate roll-call records · FEC (federal campaign finance, current two-year cycle; PAC sectors are AI estimates) · U.S. Census ZIP-to-district crosswalk · @unitedstates congress-legislators · news from approved outlets' RSS feeds, limited to a high-factuality list and matched to each bill's topic by AI; outlet leanings are approximate.

Privacy: your ZIP code and choices stay in your browser — nothing is sent or stored. Not affiliated with any government agency, party or campaign.
